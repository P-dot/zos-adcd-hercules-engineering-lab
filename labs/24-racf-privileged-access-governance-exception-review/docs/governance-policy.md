# Governance Decision Policy

**ACCEPT** — necessity demonstrated and residual risk acceptable.

**EXCEPTION** — elevated privilege is operationally/technically required but must be documented and periodically reviewed.

**REVIEW** — elevated privilege exists but evidence is insufficient to accept it permanently or remove it safely.

**REMEDIATE** — privilege has been demonstrated unnecessary, dependencies assessed, and a controlled change with rollback can be prepared.

For this simulated enterprise process, exceptions are assigned a 90-day review cycle, plus event-driven review after owner/role/subsystem/STARTED mapping/privilege changes or security incidents. The 90-day interval is a lab governance choice, not a RACF requirement.
